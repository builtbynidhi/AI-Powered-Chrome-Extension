# Demo Video Script

Target length: ~4-6 minutes.

1. **Resume upload** — open the extension popup, upload the resume (PDF),
   show the parsed structured summary (name/email/experience count/etc.)
   appearing.
2. **Land on a real Workday posting**, already logged in. Show the panel
   auto-appearing top-right with the step correctly classified ("My
   Information").
3. **Autofill step 1** — click Autofill, narrate: heuristic fields (name,
   email, phone) fill instantly with no network call; point out one
   AI-mapped custom question with its confidence badge and source-facts
   reasoning visible.
4. **Show a flagged low-confidence field** — explain it's shown as a
   suggestion only, not auto-applied, and accept or edit it manually.
5. **Show a prefilled field being kept, not overwritten** — if the posting
   pre-fills anything (e.g. from a Workday account profile), point out the
   "kept" status.
6. **Experience/Education step** — segmented date picker filling correctly,
   pill-style radio/checkbox selection, and the repeatable "Add Another"
   flow adding a second entry.
7. **File upload step** — resume re-attached automatically from the cached
   file.
8. **Review step** — walk through the `SubmitGate`: explain the three
   conditions (all steps reviewed, no unresolved validation errors, explicit
   checkbox), check the box, click Submit.
9. **Show the real submission confirmation** from Workday.
10. Brief closing note pointing at `docs/limitations.md` for what's out of
    scope and why.
